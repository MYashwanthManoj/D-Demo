Last updated 14 August 2026 · These terms apply to everyone using Career Cafe

Using Career Cafe

Career Cafe is free for students, graduates and anyone looking for entry-level work. You need to be at least sixteen to create an account. One account per person, please. Keep your sign-in details to yourself.

About the listings

We collect openings from employers' own careers pages and link you back to them. We are not the employer and we are not a recruitment agency. We check listings regularly, but employers change and close roles without telling us. If a listing looks wrong, report it and we will check the same working day. We cannot influence hiring decisions and we never promise interviews or outcomes.

Content and resources

Guides, videos, shots and roadmaps are for general education. They are written by our team and are not professional careers advice for your specific circumstances. You may share and quote our resources with attribution. Please do not republish them wholesale.

Ending your use

You can delete your account at any time. We may suspend accounts used to scrape listings, spam other users or misrepresent an employer. If we make a material change to these terms, we will tell you in the product before it takes effect.
