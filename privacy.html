Privacy policy

Last updated 14 August 2026 · Plain-language summary at the top of each section

What we collect

In short: your course, graduation year, the roles you are interested in, your location preference and, if you choose to upload one, your resume. We also record which listings you opened so the feed stays relevant and so you can find a role you looked at last week. We do not track you across other websites.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with recruiters, staffing agencies or data brokers. There is no arrangement under which a company pays us for access to students. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on Career Cafe.

Your resume

Uploading a resume is optional. It is stored so you can download it later and is never sent to an employer by us — when you apply, you upload it on the employer's own site. You can delete a stored resume at any time from Settings, and deletion is immediate.

Applications

Applications happen on the employer's site, under the employer's terms. Career Cafe does not receive, forward or store your application.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies: one to keep you signed in, and one to remember your filter preferences. Both are strictly functional. There are no advertising or profiling cookies on this product.

Your rights

You can export everything we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account outright from Settings. Deleting your account removes your profile, saved items and resume within thirty days.

Contact

Write to privacy@careercafe.app with any question and a person will reply within one working day.
